# ENG3003 individual reflection plan

## Assignment and main point

Write a first-person reflection as a member of Group 20 on how we planned, organised, monitored, and led the Case F project. T3 asks for about 1–2 pages. The reflection is worth 5% of the course grade and is due individually on Learn@PolyU (Blackboard) by 23:59 on 27 November 2026. The brief refers language and presentation to PolyU ELC's academic essay standard. [Assignment requirements, T3, pp. 2–3](<Assinment Requirement.pdf#page=2>)

**Main point.** Dividing the questions gave each member a clear starting point, and the two review rounds gave us time to use feedback and submit on time. Volunteering for the final audit gave me a wider view of my role. As the Q1 author, I was responsible for one answer. As the final reviewer, I had to consider how a reader would follow the combined report and locate the evidence behind its claims. Next time, I would keep the shared document and reviews, and agree with the group before drafting on what each section should cover. Q1 and Q3 both use IBM and Disney, which gives me a concrete example of where agreeing on the purpose of each section could make the report easier to follow. Treat this as a possible improvement based on the finished report. [Interview notes](INTERVIEW.md) · [Group report, Q1](group-report.md#question-1) · [Group report, Q3](group-report.md#question-3)

Use a natural second-year student voice and British spelling. Use “we” for group decisions and “I” for my own work, judgement, and lessons. In each main paragraph, describe a specific action and explain what it helped us do or what could have made it work better. Introduce lecture terms where they help explain the example. Keep the case background to one sentence and use the IBM and Disney examples only to explain the relationship between Q1 and Q3.
