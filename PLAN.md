## Q4. Evaluate gThanks using multiple sources

- **Purpose and design.** Keep the peer appreciation and knowledge-sharing goals. Explain why gThanks can be read as a simple form of gamification under [Deterding et al. Connect recognition to Herzberg and link prompt, specific praise to positive reinforcement.
