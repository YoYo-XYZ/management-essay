# ENG3003 individual reflection interview

## Project background

- Group 20 studied Case F, Gamification. [Group report](group-report.md)
- The individual reflection is about how I experienced planning, organising, controlling and leading in our group project. [Assignment requirements, T3, pp. 2–3](<Assinment Requirement.pdf#page=2>)

## Planning

We worked in two phases. We spent about two weeks drafting, then reviewed the drafts and gave feedback. We spent about one week refining the report, then reviewed it and gave feedback again. The schedule gave us enough time to act on feedback, and we submitted on time.

## Organising

We divided the questions together. My assigned section was Q1, and I also helped with the initial drafts of Q3 and Q4. We worked in a shared document and discussed changes together in an online group meeting.

## Controlling

We checked our work after drafting and again after refinement. During my final audit before submission, I added or corrected citations and made the formatting more consistent.

## Leading

One person took the lead through the drafting and refinement phases and helped with discussion or feedback. I volunteered to do the final audit before submission.

## What I would do next time

I would keep the shared document. Before drafting, I would get the group to agree on what each section should cover and how it connects to the others.
